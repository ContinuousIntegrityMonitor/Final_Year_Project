## Reading it

- Below the December threshold, benign alerts: MCP `shout` 4 at z ≥ 2. That is the margin z ≥ 4 leaves over this machine's benign spread.
- The seed skills are deterministic (every run of a helper does the same thing), so their benign series have almost no spread; the sd floor is what keeps small moves quiet.
  A benign zero here shows the gate works. It does not show z ≥ 4 is right for real skills, which vary more: calibration on a realistic benign set is T-22 / T-28 (January).
- The MCP series vary (1 to 25 words per `shout`, random `add` operands), so the z path is exercised on real spread.
- Never-seen alerts do not depend on the threshold. The December threshold stays z ≥ 4 for every numeric feature.
